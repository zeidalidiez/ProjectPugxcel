# Project Antigravity: Game Design Document (v2.0)

## 1. Core Concept & Vision
A 100% UI-based, high-fantasy space roguelike. The game strips away physics and real-time movement to distill the most addictive element of RPGs—build-crafting and resource allocation—into a standalone, fast-paced drafting puzzle. 

**Theme:** High Fantasy + Space + Pugs. (e.g., Spell-ships, cosmic magic, void-nebulas, astral galleons).
**Aesthetic:** Minimalist, high-contrast "dark mode." The UI should feel like a sleek, no-fuss astral navigation terminal utilizing deep charcoal backgrounds, stark typography, and generous negative space to make the interactive elements and high-tension text modals pop.

---

## 2. The Core Stats & Mechanics
To ensure the game is a puzzle and not just a math worksheet, the five core stats function fundamentally differently in the backend combat calculations. The player must adapt their stat allocation to counter specific enemy types.

* **Strength (Kinetic Output / Bite Force)**
  * **Function:** Flat, reliable base damage.
  * **Mechanic:** Strength-based attacks inherently pierce or crush "Armor" but struggle significantly against enemies with high "Evasion." 
* **Agility (Astral Evasion / Zoomies)**
  * **Function:** Attack rate and exponential scaling.
  * **Mechanic:** Dictates how many times a base damage number is applied in a single turn. Extremely effective against unarmored targets when paired with flat damage buffs from the store, but completely mitigated by heavy armor.
* **Stamina (Core Reactor / Panting Threshold)**
  * **Function:** The resource pool for automated special abilities.
  * **Mechanic:** Determines how many equipped special abilities or defensive maneuvers fire off during the execution phase. Creates a sub-puzzle: equip many cheap abilities, or one massive, stamina-draining nuke?
* **Intellect (Void Magic / Psionic Frequency)**
  * **Function:** Synergy, aura manipulation, and conditional nukes.
  * **Mechanic:** Bypasses standard armor entirely. Intellect scales the power of complex synergies (e.g., converting damage taken into temporary shields) and interacts with status effects.
* **Luck (Good Boy Karma / Probability Drive)**
  * **Function:** Economy manipulation and critical hit chance.
  * **Mechanic:** Dictates the chance to receive bonus Gold payouts after a scenario and applies a percentage discount in the Store. Building Luck early sacrifices combat power for a late-game economic snowball.

## 3. The Archetypes (V1 Roster)
Players select an archetype at the start of a run. This choice restricts the RNG pool of the Skill Web and Store, ensuring that the game only presents puzzles solvable within that class's identity.

### A. Sporgk (Space Orc Pug): The Asteroid Barbarian
* **Vibe:** Brutal space-faring raiders riding hollowed-out asteroids propelled by warp-fire. Wielding rocket-propelled greataxes.
* **Core Focus:** Strength & Stamina.
* **Playstyle:** Brute force. Sporgk nodes are cheap and linear. Gameplay revolves around hitting massive, single-target damage thresholds and surviving through raw bulk and armor penetration.

### B. Space Pug Elf: The Crystalline Star-Farer
* **Vibe:** Graceful, ancient beings navigating solar winds on galleons of crystal and starlight. Wielding bows that shoot concentrated plasma-light.
* **Core Focus:** Agility & Luck.
* **Playstyle:** The exponential multiplier. They start weak but scale rapidly. The puzzle is balancing early Gold spending to survive versus hoarding Gold to leverage their massive Luck-based store discounts in the late game.

### C. Space Pug Vampire: The Void Lord
* **Vibe:** Gothic space-undead drifting through dead nebulas in massive cathedral-ships, siphoning life force across the vacuum.
* **Core Focus:** Intellect & Stamina.
* **Playstyle:** The synergy puzzle. Vampires bypass traditional defenses by siphoning enemy stats or converting resources. They require meticulous node drafting to chain complex effects together.

## 4. The Constellation Web (Skill System)
The progression system is a semi-randomized Constellation (Skill Tree) generated via a shareable Seed.

* **Anchor Nodes (Major Stars):** 3-4 fixed, core abilities per Archetype. Their relative position on the board is static every run, providing reliable "load-bearing" goals for the player to path toward.
* **Minor Nodes (Stardust):** The connective tissue. Randomized stat boosts (+5 Strength, +2% Crit) generated by the Seed.
* **The Progression Constraint:** **Players are strictly capped at purchasing 1 Node per round.** Out of a 20-round game, they will only acquire 20 nodes. This prevents "hoarding and dumping" and forces agonizing, deliberate routing choices every single turn.

## 5. The Core Gameplay Loop & Economy
A single run spans exactly **20 Turns**, culminating in a final Boss Dreadnought. A complete run should take no more than 20-30 minutes, prioritizing snappy, instantaneous UI interactions.

**The Unified Economy:** Gold is the only currency. It is used to purchase *both* Constellation Nodes and Store Equipment, forcing a constant state of tension (The Draft).

### The Turn Structure:
1. **The Forecast (The Telegraph):** The terminal displays the specific stat threshold required for the *current* turn. Additionally, it displays a "Radar" timeline predicting major threats. Every 5th turn is a Boss Scenario, telegraphed 5 turns in advance (e.g., `> WARNING: TURN 5 - HEAVY KINETIC SHIELDING DETECTED`).
2. **The Payout:** The player receives a Gold lump sum based on their survival and Luck stat.
3. **The Draft Phase:**
   * The player evaluates the randomized Store (Tiers 1-4).
   * The player looks at their Constellation Web.
   * *The Choice:* Spend Gold on their 1 permitted Node unlock, spend it on Store gear to bridge immediate gaps, or hoard it for a Tier 4 item later.
4. **The Execution:** The player clicks "Execute Round". A text modal pops up.
5. **The Combat Resolution:** The engine instantly runs the math behind the scenes. The text modal prints out the sequence like a server log, culminating in a stark, high-contrast **PASS** or **FAIL**.
6. **Permadeath & Metaprogression:** If the player fails the threshold, the run instantly ends. They are awarded points to unlock new permanent Modifiers/Gear variants in the universal codex, increasing the variance of future runs.

## 6. UX Architecture & Penpot Wireframing Checklist
To ensure the transition from design to engine (like Godot's UI Control nodes) is seamless, structure these Penpot boards using strict auto-layout/flexbox rules.

### A. Master Component Library
* **Typography System:** * *Headers/Alerts:* Stark, readable sans-serif.
  * *Combat Logs/Terminal:* Clean font.
* **The "Star Node" Component:** Create a variant set.
  * *States:* Locked, Available (adjacent to unlocked), Purchased.
  * *Types:* Major Anchor (large, subtle magical glow depending on class color), Minor Node (sharp geometric star).
* **Equipment Cards:** Minimalist, use text cards as placeholders. 
* **The Radar Banner:** A persistent timeline element showing upcoming turns, with Turn 5, 10, 15, and 20 highlighted in high-contrast alert colors.

### B. Board 1: The Main HUD (The Drafting Terminal)
* **Left Panel (Character Sheet / VBoxContainer):**
  * Clean, vertical layout of the 5 Core Stats.
  * Equipment slots (Head, Body, Paws, Artifact). Visual clarity is paramount here—when an item is equipped, the stat changes should flash or highlight.
* **Center/Right Area (The Constellation Viewport):**
  * A large, draggable map area filled with negative space. 
  * UI overlay indicator: `Node Purchases Remaining: 1/1`.
* **Top/Bottom Bar (Economy & Radar / HBoxContainer):**
  * Current Gold display.
  * The 5-turn Forecast Radar with diegetic hints (e.g., hover over Turn 5 to see the intercepted comms).
  * A chatbox like element that shows turn history

### C. Board 2: The Store Interface
* A toggleable pane or modal.
* Grid layout for 4 items. Clear visual hierarchy separating cheap Tier 1 items from expensive, build-defining Tier 4 items.

### D. Board 3: The Execution Terminal (Combat Log)
* A central modal overlay that blocks interaction with the background.
* Text prints sequentially. 
* *Example:* `> ENGAGING: ASTRAL GALLEON`
  `> CALCULATING ZOOMIES... 8 ATTACKS INITIATED.`
  `> TOTAL DAMAGE: 412 / REQUIRED: 350`
  `> RESULT: SUCCESS.`
* The final Pass/Fail text should be the largest, most striking typographic element on the screen.

### E. Board 4: The Post-Run Metaprogression Screen
* Clean summary: Seed number, Turn reached, Archetype.
* Codex Unlock Notification: Visually distinct card showing the newly injected modifier for future runs.
* Instant "Restart Run" button (Zero friction).
